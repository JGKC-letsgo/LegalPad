# LegalPad

A matter management tool for in-house legal counsel. Built by a lawyer, for lawyers.

LegalPad gives every legal matter its own structured workspace, with deadline tracking, status at a glance, and clean summaries on demand, the kind you want two minutes before a stakeholder asks how things are going.

## Why this exists

After 16 years in-house across APAC, my matter list lived in some combination of spreadsheets, inboxes and memory. Most matter management tooling is designed for law firms billing by the hour, not for in-house teams juggling everything at once. LegalPad is my attempt at the tool I always wanted.

## Features

- **Structured workspaces** — every matter gets its own dedicated space
- **Deadline tracking** — key dates in one place, not buried in email
- **Status at a glance** — see where everything stands without opening each matter
- **Summaries on demand** — clean briefings ready when stakeholders ask
